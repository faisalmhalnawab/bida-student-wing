# BIDA Student Wing — content governance

Working implementation notes for the 2026–27 website.

## Ownership

- Nominate a Digital Lead accountable for the publishing workflow, version control and monthly content review.
- Work with the two-member National Social Media Committee and the named BIDA Executive Committee liaison.
- Use BIDA-controlled accounts and shared organisational access.
- No public programme should depend on one person's personal inbox or login.

## Publishing standard

For public events, aim to publish:
- title
- date and time
- programme stream
- speaker / faculty details once confirmed
- registration route
- access link or venue when appropriate
- status and any changes

Where dates are known, routine event pages should be published at least four weeks in advance where practicable.

## Changes

- Label programme or event changes clearly.
- Remove expired access links promptly.
- Urgent corrections should be made within two working days of verification.
- Review routine website content monthly.

## Public content standards

Apply:
- plain language
- accessibility
- copyright awareness
- consent for photographs / biographies / recordings where applicable
- data-protection principles
- consistent BIDA Student Wing branding

## Internal archive

Keep a secure, BIDA-controlled archive for:
- approved visual assets
- speaker permissions
- certificates and contribution records
- annual and achievement reports
- event evaluation
- programme documentation
- handover material
- contacts and outstanding actions

Do not publish internal working files merely because they exist in the archive.

## Definition of a complete website milestone

A page or digital-platform milestone is complete only when it is:
- live
- accurate
- accessible
- securely held by BIDA
- assigned to an accountable owner

## Handover

A digital handover is complete only when the incoming team receives the relevant:
- access
- files
- contacts
- permissions
- risks
- outstanding actions
- operational notes
